About this mockup

Plan adherence is one full-width band above the work. It never sits in the narrow step list, never folds and never scrolls away; the per-step passes moved onto the step rows as bars.

The selected step is a dossier: the brief and its passes to read on the left, the evidence (checks, commits, setup) beside it, and a dock at the foot where the decision is made.

Nothing selected looks the same as something selected: the list keeps its width and the right pane keeps its header, its two columns and its dock. It shows the review queue until you pick a step.

Click a step. J and K move, Esc comes back, the corner key hides the list.
