Which screens fill the window, which stay centred

One rule per screen, decided by what the screen shows. The width menu (Narrow, Standard, Wide, Full) replaces the Aa key everywhere a document is read or written. Dashed regions are what grows with the window.

Fill

A list, a table, a grid, a canvas or a calendar. The window is the column; more width shows more of it, never wider lines of prose.

Columns share the room: the title takes what the others leave.

Document, centred

Prose a person reads or writes. The pane around it fills; the text sits centred in it at the width chosen in the width menu, and its outline stays at the edge.

One choice holds for every document in the console.

Form, centred

Fields in a column. Labels left, fields right, centred, never stretched across a wide window. Narrow forms read faster than wide ones.

Settings, a connection's detail, a graph's setup.
